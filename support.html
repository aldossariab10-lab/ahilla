Prayer times differ by a minute or two?

Settings → Calculation method lets you choose your country's method and adjust each prayer by minutes.

The Hijri date is one day off for my country?

Ahilla uses Umm al-Qura. Adjust it by ±1–2 days in Settings → Hijri date.

Adhan notifications don't arrive?

Allow notifications in iOS Settings and open the app at least once every two or three days so it can refresh the schedule.

Why is the adhan sound short?

iOS limits notification sounds to 30 seconds, so you hear the opening of the adhan. You can change the sound in Settings → Notifications.

The weather doesn't load?

Weather needs an internet connection; everything else works offline.

Found a mistake?

In the app: More → About → Report a problem, or email us at the address above.
